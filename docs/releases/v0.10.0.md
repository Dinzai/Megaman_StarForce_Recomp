## Star Force Recomp v0.10.0

**Custom overworld sprites**
- Give any transformation its own overworld sprite: pick the form, then *Mods → Overworld sprite → Import a sprite sheet (.png)*. Mega Man walks and runs around the field as your sprite.
- Works in Leo and Zerker × Ninja, and follows the form when you switch from the in-game menu.
- The sheet is 8 rows (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) of 7 poses (standing, then 6 running frames) on a transparent background; any cell size. The app scales and reduces it to the game's 14 colours. Format in `docs/SPRITES.md`.
- *Use the recolour instead* goes back to the coloured Mega Man.

Installs over v0.9.1.
