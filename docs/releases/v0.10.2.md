## Star Force Recomp v0.10.2

**Fixed: black screen with a custom overworld sprite**
- Imported sprite sheets made Mega Man's sprite data about twice the original size. The game keeps it in memory, so busy areas could fail to load and leave a black screen. The importer now always fits the original size: it frees the tiles of Mega Man's standard poses for your art, and reuses running frames or shrinks the poses slightly when wide poses still don't fit.
- **Re-import your sheet** (or re-pick the transformation) after updating so the sprite is rebuilt.

**Decompilation, stage 1**
- New `tools/decomp.py` splits each game's code into modules and functions (about 9,300 in Leo, 10,500 in Zerker × Ninja, 11,500 in Black Ace) with a shared symbol map of everything traced so far. Plan in `docs/DECOMP.md`.

Installs over v0.10.1.
