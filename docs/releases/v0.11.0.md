## Star Force Recomp v0.11.0

**Black Ace: real transformations**
- **Mods for Black Ace → Transformation** now has 13 forms, all the game's own: **Black Ace** and **Red Joker** (Finalize), and the eleven Noise Changes (**Libra, Corvus, Cancer, Gemini, Ophiuca, Cygnus, Taurus, Virgo, Crown, Wolf, Rogue**). Mega Man starts every battle in the form, with its real moves and charged shot.
- Red Joker works in your Black Ace ROM: the game has both Finalize forms and picks one from a single byte, so you don't need a Red Joker ROM for it.
- The in-game menu can switch forms too, from the next battle.

**Black Ace: overworld**
- Each form recolours Mega Man on the field, as in the other two games.
- **Overworld sprite → Import sheet** now works in Black Ace. The game allows fewer tiles per frame than Leo, so poses come out a little smaller.

**Fixed**
- Importing a sheet with very wide poses could fail with "needs N tiles". The importer now shrinks the poses until each frame fits the game's per-frame limit.

Verified in battles started from your Black Ace save: all 11 Noise forms load, and Black Ace and Red Joker start in Finalize, fire their charged shot and win.

Installs over v0.10.2.
