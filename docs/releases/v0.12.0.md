## Star Force Recomp v0.12.0

**Black Ace: Fire Leo**
- New **Fire Leo** transformation in **Mods for Black Ace → Transformation**: Leo's battle model on Taurus Noise, the fire Noise Change, so he keeps its real moves. It needs your Leo ROM imported too.

**Fixed: some sprite sheets wouldn't import**
- Sheets where poses touch (for example a hair spike reaching the feet of the row above) were rejected because the importer only looked for fully clear gaps. It now falls back to an even 8 × 7 grid, cutting at the emptiest line near each boundary. Sheets with clear gaps import exactly as before.

Installs over v0.11.0.
