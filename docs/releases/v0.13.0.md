## Star Force Recomp v0.13.0

**Zerker × Ninja: power-ups** (Mods for Zerker × Ninja)
- **HP+1000 & Super Armor:** you own two new abilities that cost 0 Link Power: HP+1000 (+1000 max HP) and SprArmr (Super Armor). Equip them under Menu › Mega › Equip Abil.
- **Blank Cards → Mega/Giga:** every Blank Card you have turns into one of the strongest cards: PegasusMagic, LeoKingdom and DragonSky (Star Force 1's Satellite Admin Giga cards), then LeoKingdom SP, CrownThunder SP, DragonSky SP, CygnusWing SP, PegasusMagic SP, WolfWoods SP and LibraScales SP.
- **All ten cards now:** puts all ten in your CardBox straight away.

Details and the full card list: docs/POWERUPS.md.

Installs over v0.12.0.
