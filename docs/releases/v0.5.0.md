## Star Force Recomp v0.5.0

**New**
- **Transformations** (Library → Transformations): Mega Man can appear as Pegasus Magic or Dragon Sky (both in the Leo ROM) or Zerker, Ninja or Saurian (import your Star Force 2 ROM). Choose *When transforming* (replaces Leo Kingdom) or *Always in battle*. This is a visual swap for now; moves and element stay Leo's. Black Ace and Red Joker need a Star Force 3 ROM.
- **Voxel mod in game** (Settings): the top screen is drawn live as 3D voxels. Drag it to orbit. The style comes from the installed visual mod.
- **Save export/import** (Settings): back up `battery.sav` or move it between devices.
- Mods can replace single archive entries (`"entries"`), so a mod doesn't have to ship whole game files.

**Please test:** send an exported save from your first battle, and one from after unlocking Leo Kingdom if you have it. With those I can check the forms in battle and trace the real Pegasus/Dragon form switch.

Updates from v0.4.1 install over the top.
