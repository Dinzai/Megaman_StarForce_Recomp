## Star Force Recomp v0.5.3

**Fixes for the AYN Thor and other dual-screen devices**
- The bottom DS screen now stays on the second display. Before, tapping it and then pressing a button closed that window and moved both screens to the top display. The second-screen window no longer takes button focus, ignores Back, and reopens if it is ever closed.
- The voxel mod now applies to the **bottom screen** by default, where Star Force 1's field is. *Settings → Voxel screens* still offers both or top.

Installs over v0.5.2.
