## Star Force Recomp v0.7.0

**A cleaner app**
- A new launcher: pick the game (Leo, Zerker × Ninja or Black Ace), pick its mods and press Play. Controls, saves and ROM imports are under Settings (⚙).
- The app has its own icon, including a themed icon on Android 13+.
- The texture gallery, 3D model viewer and voxel preview are gone, so the app is about playing.

**All eight transformations in one system (Leo)**
- *Mods for Leo → Transformation*: Ice Pegasus, Fire Leo, Green Dragon, Zerker, Ninja, Saurian, Black Ace or Red Joker. Each is a real transformation with its own charged attack, element and model. For example, Black Ace fires spinning rings, Saurian a missile cannon and Zerker a ground-wave slash.
- Switch forms without quitting: in-game menu → *Transformation*. The new form appears from the next battle.
- Zerker, Ninja and Saurian models come from your imported Zerker × Ninja ROM; Black Ace and Red Joker from your Black Ace ROM.

**Custom battle cards (Leo)**
- *Mods for Leo → Battle cards*: turn on card packs or import your own `.json` pack. A custom card gets a new name, damage, element and picture, and keeps the attack of the card it replaces.
- Three packs are included: Elemental Cannons, Elemental Swords and Test Cards. The format is in `docs/CARDS.md`.

**Voxel 3D mode, rebuilt**
- Heights now come from the actual scene instead of pixel brightness, so the picture no longer shimmers. Floors stay flat, characters stand up as solid figures, and battles keep their real 3D depth.
- Both screens use a tilted 3D camera. Taps on the 3D touch screen still land where they look.

Installs over v0.6.0. Transformations set up in older versions are switched off; pick one again under *Transformation*.
