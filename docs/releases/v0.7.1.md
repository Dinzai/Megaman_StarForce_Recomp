## Star Force Recomp v0.7.1

**Custom battle cards in Zerker × Ninja**
- *Mods for Zerker × Ninja → Battle cards* works the same way as in Leo: turn on card packs or import your own `.json` pack. Each custom card gets a new name, damage, element and picture.
- Two packs are included: Elemental Cannons and Test Cards. Card packs now say which game they're for (`"game": "zerker-ninja"`); see `docs/CARDS.md`.

Installs over v0.7.0. Real transformations for Zerker × Ninja are still in progress.
