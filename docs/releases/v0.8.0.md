## Star Force Recomp v0.8.0

**Real transformations in Zerker × Ninja**
- *Mods for Zerker × Ninja → Transformation*: Mega Man starts every battle in a real **Tribe On**: **Zerker**, **Ninja**, **Saurian** or **Tribe King**. It's the game's own Tribe On, with all of that tribe's moves and effects.
- The other games' forms work here too, as looks on a matching tribe:
  - Ice Pegasus uses Zerker's moves.
  - Fire Leo uses Saurian's fire moves.
  - Green Dragon uses Ninja's wood moves.
  - Black Ace and Red Joker use Tribe King's moves.
- You can switch forms from the in-game menu, starting from the next battle, just like in Leo.
- Pegasus, Leo and Dragon looks need your Leo ROM imported; Black Ace and Red Joker need your Black Ace ROM.

Thanks for the Thor save: it made tracing the Tribe On code possible. Installs over v0.7.1.
