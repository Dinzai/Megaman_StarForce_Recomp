## Star Force Recomp v0.9.0

**The overworld matches your transformation**
- With a transformation on, Mega Man on the Wave Road and in EM areas is recoloured to match the form: gold for Zerker, green for Ninja and Dragon, red for Saurian, Fire Leo, Tribe King and Red Joker, ice blue for Pegasus, black for Black Ace.
- Works in Leo and Zerker × Ninja. Pick (or re-pick) a transformation to apply it; it shows from the next area you enter.

**Status Strike battle chips**
- A new card pack for each game with status-effect chips:
  - Leo: FrostFist (freezes), VenomFist (poisons), VoltFist (paralyzes), BubbleTrap (bubbles), ToxicInk (poison panels), FrostInk (ice panels).
  - Zerker × Ninja: StunBlast and StunWrap (paralyze), BubbleTrap (bubbles), ToxicCarpt and VenomStorm (poison), FrostCarpt and FrostStorm (ice).
- Turn the pack on under *Mods → Battle cards*.

Installs over v0.8.0.
